summary: "Combining language and pointing under uncertainty to ground human instructions, ask for clarification, and plan robot manipulation."
abstract: >-
  MIGU grounds multimodal human instructions by combining language with uncertain pointing gestures.
  The framework models geometric uncertainty in 3D and fuses it with semantic priors from a
  vision-language model to form a belief over intended objects and regions. This belief helps
  the robot decide whether to act or request clarification, then supplies goals for mobile
  manipulation and tabletop task-and-motion planning. Real-world evaluations demonstrate
  improved grounding over the evaluated baselines and show the benefit of explicitly
  modeling uncertainty across modalities.
